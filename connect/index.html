Connect your WHOOP

Your coach uses CoachKonnekt to plan training around how recovered you are. Connecting takes about a minute, and you don't need a CoachKonnekt account.

  • You sign in on WHOOP's own website. CoachKonnekt never sees your password.
  • Your coach sees your recovery, sleep, strain, workouts and body measurements. Nobody else does.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the WHOOP app, under connected apps.

On the next screen, WHOOP will ask you to approve access for CoachKonnekt.

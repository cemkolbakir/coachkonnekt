How it works

From wrist to training plan

Three steps, then it runs on its own.

  1. The coach shares a team link

    Each team has its own private link.

  2. The athlete connects WHOOP

    They approve access on WHOOP's own page. No CoachKonnekt account or app needed.

  3. Data arrives automatically

    New recovery, sleep and workouts reach the board within minutes of WHOOP scoring them.

For athletes

Get more from your WHOOP

Your WHOOP already knows how ready you are. Sharing it lets your coach plan training around you.

  • Training that fits how you feel. Push on the days your body is ready, ease off when recovery is low, instead of following the same plan as everyone else.
  • Warning signs caught early. Your coach can see strain building up or sleep slipping over several days and adjust before it turns into fatigue or injury.
  • A head start on every conversation. Your coach already knows how you slept and recovered, so your check-ins can go straight to how you feel and what you need.
  • Better conversations about rest. Talk about sleep, travel and recovery days with your own numbers, not guesswork.

You stay in control: only your coaching staff can see your data, it is never sold, and you can disconnect at any time from your WHOOP account. Privacy policy →
